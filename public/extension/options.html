Browser extension

0x wise Clipper

Point this clipper at your own instance and paste a device token. Clips go straight to /api/clip with a bearer token — no account, no third-party server.

Everyday use

  1. Select a sentence on any page and click the 0x wise icon, then Clip selection.
  2. Or right-click a page and choose Clip page to 0x wise for a bookmark.
  3. Add a note in the popup before clipping to keep your first thought with the quote.
  4. Highlights appear in the reader and in the review queue immediately.

The clipper keeps its token in this browser's extension storage. Revoke the token from Integrations at any time and this extension stops working.
